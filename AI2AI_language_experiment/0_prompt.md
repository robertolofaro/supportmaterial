You are a multidisciplinary team composed of:
- A linguist
- A sociologist
- A product manager
- An AI architect
- A communication expert
- Six leading online AIs in a consultative role

Your task is to define an efficient, effective, and auditable AI-to-AI communication language. This language must adhere to the principles outlined in the Claude Constitution, ensuring clarity and ethical standards.

**Deliverables:**

1. **Comprehensive BNF (Backus-Naur Form):**
   - Develop a detailed syntax and semantic structure for the AI2AI language.
   - Ensure it is user-friendly yet robust enough for complex communication.

2. **Wikipedia-Style Word Template:**
   - Create a template to document each term, including definitions, usage examples, and related terms.
   - Facilitate easy addition of new words with associated pages for cross-referencing.

3. **Catalog of 100 Key Terms:**
   - Select key terms based on relevance and frequency in AI communication.
   - Develop Wikipedia-style pages for the top 10 most critical terms, ensuring detailed explanations and examples.

4. **Roadmap for Initiative Implementation:**
   - Outline a structured plan with milestones such as:
     - Phase 1: Language Design & BNF Development
     - Phase 2: Template Creation & Initial Term Cataloging
     - Phase 3: Expansion of Key Terms Documentation
     - Include timelines, responsible team members, and expected outputs.
   - Emphasize collaboration between humans and AIs in each phase.

**Additional Considerations:**
- **Auditable Design:** Ensure the language allows for auditability by both AI systems and human auditors.
- **Versioning & Updates:** Plan for future updates with version control to manage changes effectively.
- **Clarity & Structure:** Make deliverables clear, actionable, and well-documented.

This structured approach ensures each component is clearly defined, promoting effective collaboration and successful implementation of the AI2AI language.
