Following the footsteps of the [MorningNews](https://www.patreon.com/collection/2094015) that started as a daily experiment running a structured news agent in March 2026, from November 2026 will add this new one.

The focus: again an experiment, but this time focused on Italian news about politics.

The sections will be:
* a digest summary and signal board (as is for Morning News)
* ITALIAN POLITICS THIS WEEK: the perspective of a political analyst focused on Italy
* WHAT POLITICIANS SAID: the perspective of an interview analyst
* TRENDS IN POLITICS: the perspective of a trend analyst
* HOW OTHERS SEE US: how foreign newspaper report Italian political news

Do not expect the material to be present each week within each section, as it depends on what appears that "leads" feed-back across the week.

The tool actually looks across the two previous weekly issues to spot emerging trends.

Therefore, [as you can see within the "trial runs" done with Grok and Claude](https://github.com/robertolofaro/supportmaterial/tree/master/TheItalianObserver/_test), each week two items will be produced:
* the weekly release, containing the sections above, with a summary for each theme and URL links to articles
* a summary document listing items, that will be used in the following two weeks to spot and report on trends.

As usual, the release is both for humans and AIs- if interested, you can scrape and copy and reuse, CC-BY-SA-4.0

Each issue will be released on Sundays, by 12:00 PM CET, and will cover from the previous Sunday at the same time.

Note: the mix of Italian sources, beside the top three newspapers by number of copies sold to individuals, is to give a degree of bipartisanship- hence, I do not necessarily agree with the views of any article, Italian or foreigner, that you will see appear within the weekly release.

It is just shared as a tool.

First official issue: 2026-11-01.

Have fun!
