### 1. Security decree extends preventive detention to minors 🔴
**Alert:** 🔴
**Summary:** The Council of Ministers approved a new security decree on the evening of 20 October, after violent clashes at demonstrations, notably in Milan. It creates an offence of violent participation in a demonstration, punishable by up to five years, extends preventive detention to minors, and fines parents of minors involved. According to Il Fatto Quotidiano, the text was revised after talks with the Quirinale, and a plan to give the military judicial-police powers was dropped. Meloni was quoted as saying "non è protesta, è criminalità".
**Institutional stage:** Approved by the Council of Ministers on 20 October 2026. The sources do not state the publication date in the Gazzetta Ufficiale or the parliamentary conversion deadline.

### 2. Meloni refuses to sign magistrates' pay decree 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡
**Summary:** On 18 October Palazzo Chigi sources said the Prime Minister would not sign the DPCM releasing salary adjustments for magistrates for 2018–2023, which follow Consiglio di Stato rulings and total about €1 billion, according to ANSA. The magistrates' association (ANM) reacted sharply; its deputy secretary Stefano Celli said: "Il governo decide, ma se ci sono dei margini faremo un ricorso". ANSA reported that Palazzo Chigi remains open to meeting the ANM.
**Institutional stage:** Executive decision not to adopt a DPCM; a possible appeal by the magistrates was announced but not filed, per the sources.

### 3. Budget 2027: majority summits without a final deal 🟢
**Alert:** 🟢
**Summary:** Meloni held a second evening summit on the 2027 budget at Palazzo Chigi on 22 October with Giorgetti, Tajani, Salvini and Lupi, without a final synthesis, according to ANSA. Priorities named were families, young people, the elderly, health and energy costs. Options under discussion included extending the Irpef cut up to €55,000 and lower withholding for young workers; an expected €5 billion contribution from banks and financial firms remained open. The Council of Ministers was targeted for the following week.
**Institutional stage:** Draft stage within the government; the bill had not been approved by the Council of Ministers by the cutoff.
**Sources:**
