### 1. Salvini reconfirmed Lega secretary; pledges stop to Ukraine arms 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡  
**Summary:** At the Lega extraordinary congress on 25 October Matteo Salvini was re-elected federal secretary with approximately 76 % of the vote (465–146 against Massimo Garavaglia). In his acceptance remarks he stated that the party would press the government to halt further military and financial aid to Ukraine. The declaration produced immediate tension with allies; sources close to Palazzo Chigi reported that Giorgia Meloni had contacted Salvini to urge him not to “complicate things”.  
**Institutional stage:** Party congress completed; no formal government act yet required. Next relevant step is any new decree authorising 2027 Ukraine assistance (current umbrella expires 31 December 2026).

### 2. New electoral law (Stabilicum) finalised in Parliament 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡  
**Summary:** Parliament completed the final approval of the new electoral law, commonly referred to as the Stabilicum or Melonellum. The text introduces a majority bonus for coalitions reaching 42 % and revises signature thresholds and preference rules. The measure passed the Chamber by secret ballot after Senate amendments. Attention now turns to possible challenges before the Constitutional Court and to the practical effects on the next general election.  
**Institutional stage:** Law approved by both chambers; awaiting promulgation and any referral to the Corte costituzionale.
