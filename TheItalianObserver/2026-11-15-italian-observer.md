### 1. Meloni summons opposition to Palazzo Chigi after mass school bomb-threat emails 🔴
**Alert:** 🔴
**Summary:** On 11–12 November thousands of Italian schools received email bomb threats attributed to an account linked to “Mikhail Andropov” of the “Misogynistan”. Hundreds of institutes evacuated pupils. Giorgia Meloni convened opposition leaders at Palazzo Chigi for a two-hour meeting. A joint operational protocol was issued: in the absence of concrete elements beyond a mass email, prefects decide on evacuation. Meloni pledged an urgent parliamentary briefing. Schlein, Conte and others offered collaboration but rejected any blank cheque and called for more resources on cybersecurity rather than weapons systems. Salvini stated there was no evidence of a Russian track.
**Institutional stage:** Protocol adopted and published by the Presidency of the Council; parliamentary briefing promised; investigations ongoing.

### 2. Government reverses school staff cuts in 2027 budget amid internal and opposition pressure 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡
**Summary:** The government withdrew planned reductions in school staff numbers from the 2027 budget law after sustained pressure from the Lega (Valditara, Salvini) and opposition criticism. Giancarlo Giorgetti informed the unions. The move is presented as removing an argument for forthcoming street protests (CGIL on 20 November, further mobilisations on 5 December). Majority sources describe it as avoiding avoidable internal conflicts while the parliamentary examination continues. Opposition leaders, including Schlein, continue to demand a broader rewrite of the law.
**Institutional stage:** Budget law under parliamentary examination; targeted amendments being prepared by the majority.

### 3. Giorgetti signs Ukraine aid package; Lega signals further resistance on December decree 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡
