### 1. Campo largo holds first programme call; AI and technology among early points 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡
**Summary:** On 7 November the first joint programme call of the centre-left forces took place. *Il Messaggero* reported early points of convergence on a dedicated minister for artificial intelligence and technology and on the creation of a public research centre for AI modelled on CERN. The call follows the 30 October joint note by Elly Schlein, Giuseppe Conte, Angelo Bonelli and Nicola Fratoianni that fixed the programme for the end of November, a national mobilisation for December and primaries for February 2027. The tables are open to other parliamentary opposition forces that share the goal of an alternative to the centre-right.
**Institutional stage:** Programme drafting under way; no formal document yet deposited.

### 2. Altieri withdraws from ANAC presidency after opposition challenge on incompatibility 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡
**Summary:** Trifone “Nuccio” Altieri, designated by the Council of Ministers as president of the National Anti-Corruption Authority, withdrew his availability on the evening of 6 November. Opposition parties cited Article 13 of Legislative Decree 150/2009, which prohibits appointment of persons who have held party offices in the three preceding years. The Lega stated that Altieri had never effectively assumed the Defence department role. Government sources said the two main opposition parties had been informed in advance without raising objections. Altieri cited “political, institutional and personal opportunity.”
**Institutional stage:** Designation withdrawn; new procedure required.

### 3. Government challenges Veneto end-of-life law; Salvini and Calderoli dissent 🔴
**Alert:** 🔴
**Summary:** On 5 November the Council of Ministers decided to challenge the Veneto regional law on medically assisted suicide before the Constitutional Court. Deputy Prime Minister Matteo Salvini did not take part in the vote; Regional Affairs Minister Roberto Calderoli voted against the challenge. The law, approved in September by a centre-right majority in Veneto, is the first of its kind in a centre-right-led region. President of the Veneto Regional Council Luca Zaia respected the governmental decision while defending the regional text.
**Institutional stage:** Challenge lodged; pending before the Constitutional Court.

### 4. 2027 Budget law under parliamentary examination after Council approval 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡
**Summary:** The fifth budget law of the Meloni government, valued at approximately €26 billion for 2027, was approved by the Council of Ministers on 28 October and is now under examination in the Chamber. Principal measures include the raising of the upper limit of the 33 % IRPEF bracket to €55,000, family and birth-rate support, and an increase in the National Health Fund. Opposition parties continue to criticise the text and have linked it to the planned December mobilisation.
**Institutional stage:** Bill before the Chamber; conversion target 31 December 2026.
**Sources:**
