### 1. Stabilicum electoral law enters into force 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡  
**Summary:** Law 180/2026 (Stabilicum) entered into force on 24 October 2026, fifteen days after publication in the Official Gazette. It replaces the Rosatellum with a proportional system carrying a 3 % threshold and a fixed majority bonus (70 deputies and 35 senators) for any coalition that exceeds 42 % in both chambers, subject to seat ceilings. Preferential voting is retained with blocked list leaders. The reform had received final Chamber approval on 8 October and presidential promulgation on 9 October.  
**Institutional stage:** Law in force; challenges expected before the Constitutional Court.
