### 1. Centre-left Campo largo agrees programme by end of November and primaries in February 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡  
**Summary:** Leaders of PD, M5S and AVS held a three-hour video call on 30 October and issued a joint note. They committed to a common programme by the end of November, a national mobilisation against the Meloni government in December, and primaries for the coalition candidate by February 2027. The tables are open to other parliamentary opposition forces. The timetable anticipates a possible Constitutional Court ruling and early elections in spring 2027.  
**Institutional stage:** Joint political agreement among opposition parties; no formal parliamentary act. Programme working groups to begin the following week.

### 2. 2027 Budget law reaches the Chamber amid criticism over school and health cuts 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡  
**Summary:** The 2027 Budget law (manovra) was transmitted to the Chamber after final majority talks. Opposition parties and the CGIL criticised measures including the cut of approximately 7,000 school posts (teachers and ATA staff) and insufficient additional resources for regional health services, particularly screening and mental-health programmes. The government has highlighted measures for families, young people and targeted tax relief. Parliamentary examination is expected to begin with hearings in early November.  
**Institutional stage:** Bill transmitted to the Chamber of Deputies; conversion session under way. Hearings anticipated between 9 and 12 November.

### 4. Challenges to Stabilicum electoral law advance; first hearing set for 16 December 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡  
**Summary:** Recourse proceedings against Law 180/2026 (Stabilicum) have been filed. Il Sole 24 Ore reported that the first hearing is scheduled for 16 December. The law, which entered into force on 24 October, introduced a proportional system with a majority bonus and preferential voting. Opposition parties and some majority figures had previously flagged constitutional concerns, particularly regarding the Senate majority bonus.  
**Institutional stage:** Recourses lodged with the Constitutional Court; first hearing fixed for 16 December 2026.
