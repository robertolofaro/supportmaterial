### 3. World Bank critical minerals financing 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡
**Summary:** World Bank President Ajay Banga announced on 16 October a target to quintuple the institution’s critical minerals business within five years. The new Metals and Minerals implementation plan aims for $19 billion in financing over fiscal 2026–2030, up from $3.6 billion in the prior five-year period. Banga identified 20 partner countries and noted the launch of first country compacts with other multilateral development banks to support local processing and value retention.
**Significance:** Accelerated public financing for critical minerals seeks to address supply chain vulnerabilities for energy transition technologies while supporting developing-country industrialisation.
**Sources:**
