### 1. 🔴 Overnight Russian barrage kills at least 12 across Ukraine on Putin’s 74th birthday
**Alert:** 🔴 Armed escalation
**Summary:** Russian missiles and drones struck Kyiv and at least a dozen other regions overnight into Wednesday, coinciding with Vladimir Putin’s 74th birthday. Ukrainian officials reported at least 12 civilians killed, including three children, and more than 40 wounded; later accounts from President Volodymyr Zelensky and wire reports put the toll at 20–24, with deaths in Pryluky (Chernihiv region), Dnipro, Kremenchuk, Kyiv and Oleksandriya. Zelensky said a residential building in Pryluky was destroyed as a birthday “gift” to Putin. Ukraine’s air force counted at least 50 missiles and 130 drones; Russia’s defence ministry said the strike hit industrial sites linked to missile and drone production, including Fire Point facilities. An unexploded Iskander was photographed in a Kyiv residential district. Zelensky said Ukraine had struck targets in Perm, Samara and Astrakhan in the previous 24 hours, and referred to “a response in the Black Sea” without detail.
**Frontline / strike note:** Ballistic missiles remain the gap in Ukrainian air defence. Kyiv has been under near-continuous alerts as winter approaches, with scheduled power cuts already in force.

### 13. 🔴 Kenya confirms its first Ebola death; DRC outbreak exceeds 8,300 cases and 4,000 deaths
**Alert:** 🔴 Public-health escalation
**Summary:** Kenya confirmed its first Ebola case after a Kenyan national who had lived in the Democratic Republic of the Congo for seven years died in Nairobi on Monday. **Public-health note:** The case reached a regional air hub used by aid traffic. Kenya says it has screened more than 650,000 travellers and tested 267 samples, with one positive.
