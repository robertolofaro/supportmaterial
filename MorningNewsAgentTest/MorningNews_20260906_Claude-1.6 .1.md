### 1. US strikes three Iranian oil tankers after IRGC missile attack on Navy warships 🔴
**Alert:** 🔴
**Summary:** US Central Command said its forces struck three Iranian crude oil tankers on Saturday — off Kharg Island, near Jask, and in the Gulf of Oman — after Iran's Revolutionary Guard Corps fired ballistic missiles at a US aircraft carrier and a guided-missile destroyer patrolling the Gulf. CENTCOM said both warships evaded the attacks and no US personnel were hurt; it described the tankers as part of a shadow network funding the IRGC and its regional proxies. Iranian state media said a tanker was separately hit near Kharg Island by US missiles. The exchange is the first direct US–Iran fire in roughly a month, in a war that has run since 28 February and reportedly cost 18 US service members' lives to date.
**Significance:** The strikes confirm the ceasefire lull of recent weeks has broken down, with both sides again targeting maritime and energy assets — the primary channel through which the conflict is transmitting to global oil and shipping markets.
