### 7. 🟡 Council agrees partial CMO position for MFF 2028-2034
**Summary:** The Council adopted a partial negotiating position on updated rules for the Common Market Organisation for agricultural products as part of the 2028-2034 Multiannual Financial Framework talks. The position modernises sectoral interventions (mandatory for apiculture, fruit and vegetables, and wine in certain cases), creates a dedicated protein-crop sector, maintains the EU school scheme with healthier-food goals, and allows marketing standards for additional products. Financial and horizontal elements remain tied to the overall MFF negotiation. Separately, the Council adopted a recommendation on tackling housing exclusion and homelessness and an assistance measure under the European Peace Facility for Nigeria.
**Legislative status:** Partial mandate opens trilogues with Parliament; MFF package still under negotiation.
