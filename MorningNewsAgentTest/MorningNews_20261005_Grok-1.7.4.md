### 5. 🟡 FAO Food Price Index rises 1.5% in September to 136.0
**Summary:** The FAO Food Price Index averaged 136.0 points in September 2026, up 2.0 points (1.5%) from the revised August level and 5.8% higher year-on-year. Gains were driven by cereals (+5.1%, wheat and maize at multi-year highs amid Black Sea logistics constraints and US yield concerns), sugar (+6.1%) and vegetable oils. Meat declined and dairy was stable. The index remains well below its 2022 peak.
**Market signal:** Upward pressure on global food inflation; Black Sea and weather risks remain key.
