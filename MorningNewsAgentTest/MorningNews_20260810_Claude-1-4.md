### 1. EU postpones AI Act high-risk obligations to December 2027 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡
**Summary:** The Council and Parliament reached a provisional agreement postponing the AI Act's high-risk system obligations — covering biometrics, employment, education, essential services, and migration/asylum/border management — from their original 2 August 2026 start date to 2 December 2027, as part of the Digital Omnibus package. Transparency-rule grace periods for AI-generated content were also shortened from six months to three, with a new deadline of 2 December 2026. Regulatory sandbox deadlines shift to 2 August 2027.
**Analyst note:** The 16-month delay gives providers of high-risk AI systems substantially more runway before dedicated AI Act governance requirements bite, though GDPR and sector rules still apply in the interim — a medium-term competitiveness trade-off Brussels has explicitly linked to the Draghi competitiveness agenda.

### 2. Nvidia in talks for $250bn OpenAI data-centre backstop 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡
**Summary:** Nvidia is reportedly in discussions with OpenAI on a financing backstop of up to $250bn to support a 10-gigawatt data-centre campus in Pike/Piketon County, Ohio, being developed by SB Energy, a SoftBank subsidiary.
