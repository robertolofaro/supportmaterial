### 2. 🔴 US-Denmark-Greenland security deal expands military footprint
**Summary:** On 22 September at the UN General Assembly, the United States, Denmark and Greenland signed a security agreement expanding the 1951 defence treaty. The pact permits two new US military sites (Narsarsuaq south, Mestersvig east), modernisation/expansion of Pituffik Space Base, broad overflight, landing and undersea access across Greenland and its territorial waters, and a “Golden Dome” missile-defence concept. Non-NATO states are barred from military installations or major influence over sensitive sectors without unanimous US-Denmark-Greenland approval. Trump described the outcome as giving the US “permanent control over security and all other needs.” Danish and Greenlandic leaders emphasised respect for Greenlandic self-determination and sovereignty within the Kingdom of Denmark. NATO and EU statements welcomed the de-escalation of the earlier annexation rhetoric.  
**Significance:** Ends months of alliance tension; hardens Arctic exclusion of adversaries while formalising expanded US basing. Investment-screening clauses extend security logic into economic domains.  
**Sources:**

### 11. 🟡 FAO Food Price Index rises 1.9% in August
**Summary:** The FAO Food Price Index averaged 133.3 points in August 2026, up 2.5 points (1.9%) from the revised July level and 2.5% higher than a year earlier. All five commodity groups rose. Sugar led with an 11.9% monthly jump on weather-related supply concerns in the EU, Asia and Brazil. Cereals rose 2.2% (highest since May 2024) on Black Sea logistics uncertainty, European drought impacts and US yield worries. Vegetable oils, meat and dairy also increased. The index remains 16.8% below its March 2022 peak.  
**Significance:** Broad-based firming after earlier softness; sugar spike is the standout monthly move.
