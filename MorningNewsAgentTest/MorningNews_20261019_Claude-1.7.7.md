### 10. China Proposes Export Controls on AI Models, Weights and Chip Designs 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡
**Summary:** China's commerce and science ministries proposed on 16 October adding 40 technologies to the export-control list, 23 of them AI, including large models, agents, chip designs and computing clusters; model weights and source code enter scope for the first time, Bloomberg reported. Controls would cover models of at least 2 trillion parameters or ranking in the world's top 10. Per AP and Bloomberg, Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 and Alibaba's Qwen 3.8-Max exceed that size; the draft names no model and cites no benchmark. API, app and web access and open-source code are exempt. Comments close 15 November; pre-export registration took effect 16 October.
**Analyst note:** Over 12–24 months, licensing of weights and source code would turn Chinese open-weight releases from a default export into a case-by-case licensed one, fragmenting the open-model ecosystem that foreign developers currently draw on.
