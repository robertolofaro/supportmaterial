### 8. EU AI Act Omnibus VII: High-Risk Compliance Pushed to December 2027, CSAM Ban Added 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡
**Summary:** The Council of the EU and the European Parliament reached a provisional political agreement on Digital Omnibus VII on 7 May (concluded at 04:30 CET after overnight negotiations). The central change: stand-alone high-risk AI systems under Annex III — covering biometrics, employment, education, critical infrastructure, law enforcement, and border management — see their compliance deadline shift from 2 August 2026 to 2 December 2027. AI systems embedded in regulated products under Annex I (medical devices, toys, machinery) are deferred to 2 August 2028. A new prohibition on AI-generated CSAM and non-consensual intimate imagery ("nudifier" apps) was added, with compliance by 2 December 2026. Formal endorsement by Council and Parliament expected in coming weeks; OJ publication and entry into force expected by late July 2026 — before the original August 2 deadline. This is the first deliverable under the "One Europe, One Market" roadmap agreed by the three institutions.
