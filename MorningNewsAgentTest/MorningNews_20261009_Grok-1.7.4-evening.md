### 10. 🟡 Commission names 46 new strategic raw-material projects, €21.1bn capex
**Summary:** The Commission on Friday selected 46 Strategic Projects under the Critical Raw Materials Act, across 16 member states, from 102 applications. Eight are extraction, 11 processing, 19 recycling and eight integrated. They cover 15 of 17 strategic materials, including copper, lithium, nickel, cobalt, manganese, graphite, rare earths, magnesium and tungsten. Expected capital need is about €21.1bn. The Commission said it had already mobilised more than €2bn in public funding for the earlier list; developers have warned that projects chosen in 2025 could still fail without further money. Named schemes include Zinnwald Lithium in Germany, Skouries in Greece, Arctial’s planned aluminium smelter in Finland and Viscaria copper in Sweden. Le Monde reported six of the projects are in France.
**Legislative status:** Selection under the existing CRMA, not a new legal act. Permitting fast-track and funding access follow; the financing gap is unresolved.
