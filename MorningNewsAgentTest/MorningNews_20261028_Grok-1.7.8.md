### 8. Eurostat records EU fertility at historic low of 1.34 🟢
**Alert:** 🟢
**Summary:** Eurostat reported on 27 October that the EU total fertility rate fell to 1.34 live births per woman in 2024 — the lowest level since the series began in 2001 and well below the 2.10 replacement rate.
