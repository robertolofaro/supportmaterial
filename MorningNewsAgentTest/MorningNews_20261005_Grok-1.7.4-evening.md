### 12. Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine — optogenetics 🟢
**Alert:** 🟢
**Summary:** The Nobel Assembly at Karolinska Institutet awarded the 2026 medicine prize jointly to Karl Deisseroth (Stanford / Howard Hughes), Peter Hegemann (Humboldt University, Berlin) and Georg Nagel (Würzburg) "for their discoveries concerning light-gated ion channels and optogenetics". Hegemann and Nagel identified channelrhodopsin in a single-celled alga; Deisseroth turned it into a light-controlled switch for nerve cells in a living brain. The prize is 12 million Swedish kronor, shared equally. The Commission noted Hegemann as an EU-funded researcher. Physics, chemistry, literature, peace and economics prizes follow this week; the peace prize is due on Friday.
**Analyst note:** Optogenetics is now foundational lab infrastructure rather than a speculative tool; the award ratifies a method already embedded in circuit neuroscience.
