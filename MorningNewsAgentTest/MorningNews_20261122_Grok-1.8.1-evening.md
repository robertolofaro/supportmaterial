**Alert:** 🟡
**Summary:** European Council President António Costa presented a revised negotiating box for the 2028–2034 Multiannual Financial Framework on 21 November. The proposal is approximately 10% (€204 billion) below the Commission’s original plan, bringing the total closer to €1.78 trillion. It reduces cohesion and competitiveness envelopes while introducing potential new own resources, including a digital services levy on large technology firms. National contributions are projected to rise only marginally.
**Market signal:** Neutral to mildly supportive for EU fiscal cohesion; lower headline figure reduces immediate pressure on member-state budgets.

**Alert:** 🔴
**Summary:** The COP31 climate conference in Antalya ended on 22 November without adoption of a cover decision.
