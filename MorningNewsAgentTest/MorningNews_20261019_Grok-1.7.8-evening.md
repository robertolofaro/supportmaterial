### 10. FAO — Food Price Index 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡
**Summary:** The FAO Food Price Index averaged 136.0 points in September 2026, up 1.5% from the revised August level. **Significance:** The modest monthly increase maintains upward pressure on global food import bills for vulnerable importers while remaining well below the 2022 crisis peak.
