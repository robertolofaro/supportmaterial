### 1. Trump reinstates Hormuz blockade, demands 20% toll 🔴
**Alert:** 🔴
**Summary:** President Trump said Monday the US is "reinstating" its naval blockade of Iranian shipping in the Strait of Hormuz and, reversing prior US policy, will charge non-Iranian vessels a 20% toll for safe passage. The announcement followed fresh US strikes on Iranian targets after three tankers were attacked in the strait over the weekend. Iran's foreign minister Abbas Araghchi called the US position "absolutely right" but said Iran, as the strait's "guardian," would set a fairer rate than 20%. The International Maritime Organization said it opposed mandatory tolls on international waterways and was seeking clarification from Washington.
**Significance:** A US toll regime would mark an unprecedented militarisation of a global chokepoint carrying roughly a fifth of the world's oil, inviting retaliatory Iranian tolls and further fragmenting freedom-of-navigation norms.
