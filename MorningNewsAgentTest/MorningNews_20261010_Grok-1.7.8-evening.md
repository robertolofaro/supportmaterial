### 1. 🔴 Trump announces Russian diesel supplies after Putin call; Zelenskyy condemns
**Summary:** President Trump stated that Russia will immediately supply more than 300,000 tonnes of diesel to US and global markets, followed by 500,000 tonnes in November and further volumes, after a phone call with President Putin. The US Treasury issued a temporary general licence easing sanctions on Russian diesel through April 2027. Ukrainian President Zelenskyy called the arrangement “gifts to Putin” and said Ukrainian negotiators had been used as a smokescreen.
**Significance:** The move reverses years of US pressure on Russian energy revenues and coincides with high US diesel prices ahead of midterm elections. Volumes remain modest relative to global diesel demand but carry major political signal value.
