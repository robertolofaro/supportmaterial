### 8. EU Return Regulation 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡
**Summary:** The Council of the EU gave final approval on 1 October to a regulation establishing a common EU return system. It introduces a European Return Order and voluntary mutual recognition of return decisions, to be reassessed three years after entry into force; permits indefinite entry bans and detention beyond 24 months for people posing a security risk; and allows member states to return people to "return hubs" in non-EU countries under agreements respecting non-refoulement. Irish Justice Minister Jim O'Callaghan noted that around two in three people ordered to leave do not.
**Legislative/policy stage:** Adopted by the Council on 1 October 2026; awaiting publication in the Official Journal, with entry into force the following day. Return-hub provisions apply immediately; others apply one year after entry into force.
