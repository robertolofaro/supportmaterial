### 12. Council agrees digital omnibus mandate 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡
**Summary:** The Council agreed its position on the digital omnibus (Omnibus VII) on 21 October. It keeps the current GDPR definition of personal data, adds a pseudonymisation provision, backs a longer personal-data-breach notification deadline, and widens consent-free cookie purposes such as audience measurement and security. It replaces the proposed single EU incident-reporting entry point with a national entry point in each member state plus an EU-level information point. The AI-related half of the package was adopted on 29 June 2026.
**Analyst note:** The roadmap targets concluding all simplification packages by end-2027, but replacing a single EU entry point with national ones risks fragmenting cyber and data-breach notification over the next 12–24 months. Legislative stage: Council negotiating mandate agreed 21 October 2026; talks with Parliament start once it adopts its position.
