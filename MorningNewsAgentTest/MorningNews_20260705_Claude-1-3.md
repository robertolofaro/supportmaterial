### 2. Hormuz ceasefire holds but transit-volume trackers diverge sharply 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡
**Summary:** Shipping through the Strait of Hormuz continues its "cautious restart" following the 17 June US–Iran memorandum of understanding, but trackers disagree on how far it has come. Windward's Maritime Intelligence Operations Center recorded 39 vessels transiting on 3 July (17 inbound, 22 outbound) — about 46% of the roughly 84/day pre-war baseline — describing the corridor as "open in both directions." IMF PortWatch's most recent published figure, for 28 June, showed only 27 vessels (32% of normal). Bloomberg/Kpler-sourced reporting separately noted 35 tankers exited the strait in a single session in late June, the first return to a pre-war-typical range. Talks between US and Iranian mediators in Doha continue, delayed slightly by the funeral of former Supreme Leader Ali Khamenei, which began 4 July.
**Significance:** The scale of disagreement between trackers — and the fact that no single one shows full normalisation — means genuine confidence in Gulf energy security remains provisional even as headline oil prices fall.
