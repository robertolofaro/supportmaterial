### 15. Thwaites glacier: new study doubles sea-level estimate 🟢
**Alert:** 🟢
**Summary:** A study in Nature Communications by University of California and NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory researchers found warm seawater is penetrating beneath Thwaites glacier in West Antarctica, and that the glacier could add 30 mm (1.2 inches) to sea levels by 2100 even without further warming, double earlier estimates. It puts the chance of Thwaites unpinning from the Mouginot Ridge by century's end at 60%. Thwaites contributes about 4% of global sea-level rise and loses about 50 billion tonnes of ice a year; a July British Antarctic Survey study projects losses for at least 150 years.
**Horizon:** Long-term: multi-century sea-level commitment; near-term policy relevance lies in coastal-adaptation planning rather than emissions pathways.
**Sources:**
