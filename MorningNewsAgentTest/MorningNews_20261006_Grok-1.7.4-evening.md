### 1. Yemen / Bab el-Mandeb 🔴
**Alert:** 🔴
**Summary:** Yemen’s recognised government said on Monday its forces had retaken the Red Sea port of Mocha and positions along the Bab el-Mandeb, backed by a Saudi-led coalition that said 100 fighter jets were providing air cover. The Houthis denied losing the coast, claimed they still held recent gains, and said they had struck Riyadh airport and an Aramco site. Saudi civil aviation confirmed that King Abdullah airport in Jazan and Najran airport were hit on Monday evening, wounding three people. Pakistan’s defence minister said the role so far was reconnaissance and support, not frontline combat. Claims on the ground have not been independently verified.

### 3. French nuclear deterrent 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡
**Summary:** France’s defence ministry said on Tuesday it had conducted the first test launch of the M51.3 submarine-launched ballistic missile, without a warhead, from the SSBN Le Vigilant off Brittany.
