**Alert:** 🔴
**Summary:** According to New York Times sources cited by Kommersant, US commanders have tabled a three-day, high-intensity strike plan against Iranian drone and missile stores, energy sites and IRGC command posts. ### 9. Council adopts EU return regulation 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡
**Summary:** The Council gave final approval on 1 October to a regulation establishing a common EU system for returning people with no right to stay. It introduces a European Return Order, voluntary mutual recognition of return decisions, entry bans and detention beyond 24 months for people posing security risks, and a legal basis for return hubs in non-EU countries under agreements respecting non-refoulement. Unaccompanied minors are excluded from hub arrangements. Ireland's justice minister said around two in three people ordered to leave do not do so.
**Legislative/policy stage:** Adopted by the Council 01 October 2026; to be published in the Official Journal and enter into force the following day. Return-hub provisions apply immediately; other provisions apply one year after entry into force. Mutual recognition is reassessed three years after entry into force.
**Sources:**
