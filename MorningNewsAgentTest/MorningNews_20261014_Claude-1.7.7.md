### 12. French supermarket refrigeration cyberattack 🔴
**Alert:** 🔴
**Summary:** Hundreds of French supermarkets were hit by a cyberattack overnight on 11–12 October, according to Xinhua citing BFM Business. Affected sites are mainly Intermarché and Coopérative U outlets plus some independent shops. **Analyst note:** Remote manipulation of industrial refrigeration controls shows food-chain operational technology is now a primary cyber target; expect NIS2 and Cyber Resilience Act vendor-liability and vulnerability-disclosure obligations to be tested within 12–24 months.

### 15. Super El Niño 🟡
**Alert:** 🟡
**Summary:** A super El Niño formed in September, according to China's National Climate Center, Xinhua reports (12 October). The WMO said on 8 October that tropical Pacific sea-surface temperatures will reach record levels in October–December, peaking in December, with near-100% likelihood of persisting to February 2027 and potential to rank among the strongest since 1950. Copernicus data show a record global daily mean sea-surface temperature of 21.10°C on 22 August. UNICEF expects more than 347 million children in nearly 100 countries to face abnormal rain or dryness in October–December. Researchers say 2027 could become the hottest year on record.
**Horizon:** Medium-term (to February 2027) with a long-term warming signal — peak impacts on rainfall, harvests and health fall in the first half of 2027.
