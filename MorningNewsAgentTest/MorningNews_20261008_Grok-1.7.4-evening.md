### 1. 🔴 Russian glide bomb kills at least 30 on buses in Kramatorsk
**Summary:** A Russian guided aerial bomb struck two city buses in Kramatorsk, Donetsk region, on Thursday morning. Ukrainian authorities put the death toll at at least 30 and later 33, with 18 wounded; three further deaths were reported in the nearby village of Bilenke. Donetsk governor Vadym Filashkin said the buses were chosen to maximise civilian casualties and suspended public transport in Kramatorsk and Sloviansk. President Zelensky said the strike would not go unanswered. Zelensky separately put recent Russian strikes across Pryluky, Kramatorsk, Kyiv and other cities at 78 dead and 215 wounded. No immediate comment from Moscow.
**Operational:** Glide-bomb pattern against the Donbas “fortress belt” cities still under Ukrainian control; public transport and logistics nodes are now explicit targets.

### 14. 🟢 Anne Carson wins the Nobel Prize in Literature
**Summary:** The Swedish Academy awarded the 2026 Nobel Prize in Literature to Canadian poet, essayist and translator Anne Carson, 76, “for her bold and inventive oeuvre that, in playful dialogue with the classical tradition, has created new forms for contemporary literature”.
