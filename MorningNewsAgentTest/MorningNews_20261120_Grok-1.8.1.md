### 2. ICJ Myanmar genocide ruling 🔴
**Alert:** 🔴
**Summary:** The International Court of Justice ruled on 19 November 2026 that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya minority during 2017 military operations in northern Rakhine State. The 9-6 decision found genocidal intent, ordered safe returns and family reunifications, and held the state responsible for killings, sexual violence and village burnings. It is the first time the court has found a state guilty of genocide. An estimated 10,000 people were killed and more than 700,000 fled to Bangladesh.
**Significance:** The landmark judgment establishes a legal benchmark for state responsibility under the Genocide Convention and has been noted for potential relevance to other ongoing cases.
